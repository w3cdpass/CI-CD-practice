Global Crises Overview

Understanding major challenges facing our world today

Climate Change

Rising global temperatures, extreme weather events, and sea level rise threaten ecosystems and human communities worldwide.

Global temperature rise: +1.1°C since 1880

Poverty

Over 700 million people live in extreme poverty, struggling to meet basic needs like food, water, and shelter.

9.2% of world population lives on less than $1.90/day

Global Health

Pandemics, lack of healthcare access, and emerging diseases challenge global health systems.

COVID-19 caused ~7 million reported deaths worldwide

Conflict & Displacement

Wars and violence force millions to flee their homes, creating refugee crises.

110 million forcibly displaced people worldwide (2023)

Data sources: UN, WHO, World Bank | Last updated: 2023

For more information visit: United Nations
